Internet of Things (IoT)

🕰️ How IoT Started

The concept of IoT was introduced in 1999 by Kevin Ashton. It gained real traction with the rise of sensors, embedded systems, and connectivity advancements. The first internet-connected device was a Coke machine at Carnegie Mellon University in the 1980s.

⚙️ Evolution of IoT

  • Early 2000s: RFID and M2M communication.
  • 2010s: Smart home devices, wearables, and industrial IoT.
  • Now: Integration with AI, 5G, and edge computing.

📚 How to Learn IoT

  1. Start with Basics: Electronics, sensors, Arduino/Raspberry Pi.
  2. Networking: Learn MQTT, HTTP, and other IoT protocols.
  3. Advanced: Edge computing, IoT security, cloud integration (AWS IoT, Azure).
