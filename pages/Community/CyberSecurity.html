Cybersecurity

🕰️ How Cybersecurity Started

Cybersecurity began in the 1970s with the ARPANET, where the first known malware called "Creeper" appeared. It was followed by the first antivirus "Reaper." Over the decades, cybersecurity evolved as threats became more complex and widespread.

⚙️ Evolution of Cybersecurity

  • 1980s–1990s: Antivirus and firewalls emerged.
  • 2000s: Rise of internet-based attacks and data breaches.
  • 2010s–Now: Advanced persistent threats, ransomware, and cloud security concerns.

📚 How to Learn Cybersecurity

  1. Start with Basics: Learn networking, OS concepts, and Python/Bash scripting.
  2. Key Areas: Network security, cryptography, ethical hacking, digital forensics, and secure coding.
  3. Certifications: CEH, CompTIA Security+, OSCP.
